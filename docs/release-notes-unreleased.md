- Fixed: switching presets on a paused scene no longer starts sounds playing at full volume, and switching right after pressing Pause no longer causes a short loud swell — the scene stays silent until you press Play, and then resumes with the new preset.
- New: right-click files in the Library and choose **Add to Playlist**, **Add to Scene**, or **Add to Soundboard** to add them without leaving the Library. Each list is alphabetical, and a checkmark shows the ones that already have the files (picking one of those does nothing). **New Playlist… / New Scene… / New Soundboard…** at the top asks for a name and creates it with the selected files. A short message at the bottom of the Library confirms each add and fades on its own, so you can go straight on to previewing the next track. Sounds added to the scene that's playing arrive switched off, so nothing starts mid-session.
- New: playlists show how many tracks they have, next to the name at the top of the Playlists tab, on the right of each row in the Playlists list, and next to the title on a playlist's card in a scene. Long names in the Playlists and Scenes lists now end in "…" instead of adding a sideways scrollbar.
- Fixed: adding tracks to an empty playlist now turns on its Play and Shuffle buttons right away, instead of only after you pick the playlist again.
- Fixed: reordering, removing, or adding soundboard buttons while a sound plays no longer drops the playing button's highlight.
